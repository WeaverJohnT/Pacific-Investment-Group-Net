About us

A disciplined approach to private lending

Pacific Investment Group operates as a private lender in the hard money lending space by structuring and managing project-specific lending entities. We bring together individuals with experience in lending, real estate, foreclosure processes, escrow, finance, and entity structuring.

For each loan opportunity, a separate LLC is formed and capitalized by multiple members who contribute funds for that specific transaction.

Acting as both manager and member, we oversee the entire process — including structuring the entity, coordinating with legal and accounting professionals, underwriting the loan, and managing the deployment and recovery of funds.

This model ensures transparency, risk compartmentalization, and alignment of interests, while allowing investors to participate passively in real estate-backed lending opportunities.

New to hard money?

What is hard money lending?

Hard money loans are short-term, asset-based financing designed for real estate investors and borrowers who need fast, flexible funding. Unlike traditional banks, lending decisions focus primarily on the value and potential of the property being used as collateral — not solely on the borrower's credit profile or income history.

⚡

Speed when it matters

Real estate opportunities don't wait. Hard money loans are structured for fast approvals and quick closes — helping investors move on deals before conventional financing could even begin processing an application.

🏠

Collateral-first underwriting

The property's value and investment potential drive the lending decision. This opens doors for investors whose income or credit history wouldn't satisfy traditional bank requirements, but whose deal fundamentals are sound.

🔧

Built for investors

Fix-and-flips, new construction, bridge financing, acquisitions of income-producing properties — hard money is purpose-built for the practical realities of real estate investment, with terms tailored to each project.

🤝

Direct relationships

We work directly with borrowers, brokers, and real estate professionals. No institutional bureaucracy — streamlined underwriting, responsive communication, and customized terms rooted in transparency and shared outcomes.

Hard money vs. traditional bank loans

A quick comparison for those new to this space:

Traditional bank Hard money (our LLC loans)
Approval basis Credit score, income, debt-to-income ratio Property value, equity, and investment merit
Speed to close 30–60+ days Days to a few weeks
Loan term 15–30 years Short-term: typically 6–24 months
Flexibility Rigid, standardized requirements Customized terms per project
Best suited for Owner-occupied, long-term financing Investors needing speed and flexibility
How it works

Each deal is its own entity

Every loan opportunity is structured as a standalone investment vehicle — a single-purpose LLC tied to one transaction — providing clear separation of assets and liabilities across all participants.

01

Separate LLC per loan

Each opportunity is isolated in its own legal entity, providing full compartmentalization of risk and liability.

02

Multi-member capital

Members contribute capital specific to each transaction, participating passively with no day-to-day obligations.

03

Active management

We handle all aspects: sourcing, entity structuring, legal coordination, underwriting, and fund recovery.

04

Real property security

All loans are backed by real estate collateral, providing a tangible asset layer beneath every investment.
